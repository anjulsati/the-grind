# The Offer

**Live: https://anjulsati.github.io/placement-prep-os/**

A single-page, 22-week plan built for one goal: **a FAANG/MAANG-level offer**.

```
W1-3   16 Nov - 6 Dec    EXAMS        40 min/day, 21 easy problems
W4     7 Dec - 13 Dec    TRANSITION   last paper Mon 7th, full block from Tue 8th
W5-19  14 Dec - 21 Mar    FULL PLAN    2h 45m/day
W20-22 22 Mar - 18 Apr    SPRINT       repair, 5 mocks, portfolio, 45+ applications
```

585 tasks · 244 unique problems · 0 repeats (re-solve days are deliberate).

---

## Built to the FAANG bar

- **90% problem solving.** DSA gets the weight, not the resume.
- **Striver A2Z** to learn, **NeetCode 150** to revise — the two names that come up on every r/LeetcodeDesi thread.
- **Aditya Verma** for DP, recursion, stacks — the most-recommended DP playlist in the Indian dev community.
- **Gate Smashers** for OS/DBMS/CN — the standard.
- **Shrayansh Jain** for LLD, **Gaurav Sen** for HLD.
- Every week opens with **the video to watch first**, chosen per pattern.
- From **week 10**, every week ends with a **timed set** — 2 unseen mediums in 60 minutes. That's the FAANG online assessment, rehearsed.
- **The FAANG bar** section: the three routes in (summer 2028 internship, on-campus Aug-Dec 2027, off-campus), the interview loop, and the difficulty bar to train against.

## Two projects, one page

Every week is split into two tracks:

| | |
|---|---|
| **01 DSA** | pattern of the week · watch-first video · the problems · the day-7 test |
| **02 CS + Development** | CS topic · AI tasks · career tasks · deliverable |

## Pen mode
