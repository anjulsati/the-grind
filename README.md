# Zero to Offer

**Live: https://anjulsati.github.io/zero-to-offer/**

One page. 22 weeks. Built for one goal: a **FAANG/MAANG-level offer**.

Everything is three projects, and every week and every day is three boxes:

| | |
|---|---|
| **01 DSA** | pattern of the week · watch-first video · problems · the day-7 test |
| **02 CS** | the CS area of the week, one topic a day |
| **03 DEV** | agentic AI build tasks · career tasks · the weekly deliverable |

```
W1-3   16 Nov - 6 Dec    EXAMS        40 min/day, 21 easy problems
W4     7 Dec - 13 Dec    TRANSITION   last paper Mon 7th, full block from Tue 8th
W5-19  14 Dec - 21 Mar    FULL PLAN    2h 45m/day
W20-22 22 Mar - 18 Apr    SPRINT       repair, 5 mocks, portfolio, 45+ applications
```

600 tasks · 244 unique problems · 0 repeats · every day has a DSA, CS and DEV box.

## FAANG calibration

- Striver A2Z to learn, NeetCode 150 to revise, Aditya Verma for DP/recursion/stacks, Gate Smashers for OS/DBMS/CN, Shrayansh Jain for LLD, Gaurav Sen for HLD — the picks that come up on every r/LeetcodeDesi thread.
- Every week opens with the **video to watch first**.
- From W10, every week ends with a **timed set**: 2 unseen mediums, 60 minutes. The FAANG online assessment, rehearsed 10 times.
- **The FAANG bar** section: three routes in, the interview loop, the difficulty bar, and STAR.
